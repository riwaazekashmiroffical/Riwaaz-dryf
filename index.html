Good to know

How long does delivery take?

Usually 5-8 working days across India. Delivery is free.

How can I check if saffron is real?

Put a few threads in a glass of lukewarm water. Real saffron releases its golden colour slowly and the threads stay red. Fake saffron turns the water red quickly.

How should I store dry fruits?

Keep them in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
