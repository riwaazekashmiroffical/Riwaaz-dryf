Questions we often get

Is there a booking fee?

No. Our service is included in the trip price, so you never pay a separate fee to us.

Do I pay anything to get my plan?

No advance and no fee. The call and your custom plan are free.

When do I pay?

On arrival. In peak season, some hotels ask for a small advance to hold the room. We tell you before you confirm anything.

Can I change my dates?

Yes, free changes up to 7 days before travel, where hotels and drivers allow it. We tell you upfront if any booking cannot be changed.

I am a woman travelling alone. How does it work?

A woman from our local team receives you and stays with you at a family homestay. Before you travel we share your stay and driver details with you on WhatsApp.

Do I need permits for some places?

Some border-side areas, such as Gurez, Keran, Machil and parts of Kupwara, can need permission. We tell you what applies for your dates before you plan around it.

Is Kashmir safe to visit?

Many tourists visit every year. We check the current situation for your dates and tell you plainly if a place is not advisable then.
