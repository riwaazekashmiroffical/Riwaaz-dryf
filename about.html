Our Story

Srinagar, Kashmir · Family-run

Riwaaz-e-Kashmir is a family-run Kashmiri brand based in Srinagar. We bring the real taste of Kashmir, straight from the valley, to homes across India.

Why we started

We noticed that in many markets, low-grade dry fruits were being sold at very high prices. Buyers were paying more and getting less. We wanted to close that gap: real quality, at a fair price, with no compromise on health.

From our fields and home

Our saffron is grown in our own fields in Pampore. Our walnuts come from our own fields too, and we hand-pick the small, snow-white kernels. Our honey and kahwa are prepared by us at home. We started by selling to local mandis and to customers across India by India Post. Now we have come online so that more people can taste what Kashmir really offers.

What we offer

  • Pampore saffron from our own fields
  • Premium Kashmiri walnut kernels
  • Kashmiri kahwa
  • Kashmiri honey
  • Mixed dry fruit packs and gift boxes, including wedding gifts
  • Pashmina, pashmina-blend and traditional shawls

Our promise

Honest quality, hand-checked, with nothing mixed in. We want everyone in India to be able to enjoy the pure taste of Kashmir without paying inflated prices.

Our registrations

Riwaaz-e-Kashmir operates as a registered business with an FSSAI food licence and Udyam registration. Our trademark application has been filed and is in process.
